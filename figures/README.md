# Published figures and provenance

The four PNGs reproduce Figures 1, 2, 4, and 6 of Ma et al. (2026), *The Astrophysical Journal* 1012, 116, DOI [10.3847/1538-4357/aeb6b6](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/aeb6b6).
They were rendered at 180 dpi from pages 3, 4, 6, and 8 of the [repository PDF](../paper/Ma_et_al_2026_ApJ_1012_116.pdf), retaining the original captions. Only surrounding page text and headers were cropped; panels, labels, and scientific content were not edited.

Copyright 2026 the authors. Reused under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as stated on the article's first page. These are published reference results, not figures regenerated by the current code snapshot. Figure 4 includes MHD simulation results whose solver is not included in this repository.
